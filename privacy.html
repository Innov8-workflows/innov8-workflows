1Who we are

1.1
Innov8 Workflows ("we", "us", "our") builds and maintains websites and runs digital marketing for businesses in the UK. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.
1.2
For the information described in this policy, Innov8 Workflows is the data controller. That means we decide what is collected and why, and we are responsible for looking after it.
1.3
Innov8 Workflows is a sole trader business operated by Jamie Barlow, based in England.
1.4
For any question about this policy or your data, email jamie@innov8workflows.co.uk.

2What this policy covers

2.1
This policy covers personal information we collect through innov8workflows.co.uk, through direct contact with us by email, phone, WhatsApp or social media, and in the course of providing services to our clients.
2.2
It does not cover websites we have built for our clients. On those sites, our client is the data controller and their own privacy policy applies. Our role there is described in section 8.
2.3
Our website links to other websites. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your data, so check their own policies.

3What we collect

We collect different information depending on how you interact with us.

If you enquire with us

  • Your name, business name, email address and phone number
  • The content of your enquiry and any messages you send us
  • Information about your business relevant to the work, such as trade, service area and existing website

If you become a client

  • Billing contact details and business address
  • Records of the services we provide and the work we do for you
  • Correspondence between us, including email, WhatsApp, call notes and meeting notes
  • Login and account details for services we manage on your behalf, such as domains, email hosting, Google Business Profile and advertising accounts
  • Payment records. We never see or store your full card details. Those are handled directly by Stripe

If you visit our website

  • Your IP address, browser type, device type and operating system
  • Which pages you viewed, how long for, and where you arrived from
  • Cookie data, as described in section 10
In plain English

Broadly: what you tell us, what we need to bill you and do the work, and some basic technical information about your visit. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our systems.

8Data on our clients' websites

8.1
When someone submits an enquiry form or clicks to call on a website we built for a client, that information belongs to our client, not to us.
8.2
For that data, our client is the data controller and we act as their data processor. We handle it only on their instructions and only to keep their website running.
8.3
If you are a member of the public who contacted a business through a website we built, and you want to know what happens to your information, contact that business directly. Their privacy policy applies, not this one.
8.4
If you cannot reach them, get in touch and we will point you in the right direction.
In plain English

Leads that come through a client's website are the client's data. We only look after the plumbing. If you are a customer of one of our clients, speak to them about your information.

10Cookies

10.1
Cookies are small files stored on your device that help a website work and help us understand how it is used.
10.2
Essential cookies keep the site secure and working. These do not need your consent.
10.3
Analytics cookies tell us which pages are visited and how people move through the site, so we can improve it. We only set these if you agree.
10.4
Advertising cookies measure whether our advertising works. We only set these if you agree.
10.5
You can change your mind at any time through the cookie settings on our site, or by clearing cookies in your browser. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working.

11How we protect it

11.1

11.2
No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that information sent over the internet is entirely safe in transit, but we take reasonable steps to protect it once we have it.
11.3
If a breach happens that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and report it to the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours as the law requires.
